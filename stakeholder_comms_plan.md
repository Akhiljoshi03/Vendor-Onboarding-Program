# Stakeholder Communication Plan: Vendor Onboarding Program

**Work stream:** Product Operations — "be the point of contact for
stakeholder feedback, and document releases and internal knowledge."

## Stakeholder Map

| Stakeholder | Interest | Communication need | Channel & cadence |
|---|---|---|---|
| Cataloging Ops team | Execution owner | Daily blocker visibility | Async Slack update, daily |
| Pricing Ops team | Execution owner | Upcoming review load (avoid surprise spikes) | Shared calendar of upcoming sign-off dates, updated weekly |
| Procurement | Vendor relationship owner | Vendor-facing delays that need their intervention | Flagged directly, same-day, only when needed (not routine noise) |
| Engineering (cataloging tooling) | QC automation owner | QC error-rate trends (signal for tooling gaps) | Bi-weekly trend summary, not per-incident |
| Vendors | External party | Clear, specific feedback on rejected rows; onboarding status | Structured error report (SOP Step 3) + status email at each stage transition |
| Leadership | Business outcome owner | Is the festive-season catalog ready in time | Weekly 1-slide summary: vendors on track / at risk / blocked, with the single biggest risk called out |

## Principles
1. **Escalate by exception, not by routine.** Daily updates are terse
   (on-track/at-risk/blocked only); a detailed explanation is only written
   when something needs a decision from someone else. This keeps
   stakeholders reading updates instead of skimming past noise.
2. **One number, not ten.** Leadership's weekly update leads with "X of 12
   vendors on track for Day 10" before anything else — the answer to the
   question they actually have.
3. **Documentation lives with the process, not in someone's head.** The SOP
   (`SOP_vendor_onboarding.md`) is the single source of truth for "how this
   works," updated via its change log rather than re-explained in every
   status update — this is what "document releases and internal knowledge"
   means in practice.

## Sample Weekly Leadership Update (illustrative)

> **Vendor Onboarding — Week 2 Update**
> **Status:** 9 of 12 vendors on track for Day-10 go-live; 2 at risk, 1
> blocked.
> **Biggest risk:** Pricing Ops review backlog (3 vendors queued behind
> other launches) — escalated, expect resolution by Wed.
> **Decision needed:** none this week.
> **Next update:** Friday.

This format is deliberately short: a PM's stakeholder updates should be
skimmable in under 30 seconds, with anything that needs real attention
called out explicitly rather than buried in a wall of status text.
